Apple Cider Donut Cake With Cinnamon Glaze

October 3, 2026

There is nothing quite like the smell of warm apple cider donuts wafting through the crisp autumn air. This Apple Cider Donut Cake captures all that seasonal magic in a simple, elegant Bundt cake form. It features a moist, tender crumb infused with concentrated apple flavor and coated in a sweet, spiced glaze that mimics the sugary crunch of a fresh donut.

Servings: 12
