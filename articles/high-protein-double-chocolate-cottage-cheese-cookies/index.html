High Protein Double Chocolate Cottage Cheese Cookies

October 3, 2026

If you are looking for a way to satisfy your sweet tooth while hitting your protein goals, these High Protein Double Chocolate Cottage Cheese Cookies are about to become your new favorite snack. By using cottage cheese as a base, these cookies achieve a fudgy, brownie-like texture that is incredibly satisfying. They are rich, chocolatey, and packed with nutrients, making them perfect for a post-workout treat or a healthy dessert.

Recipe Information

Prep Time: 15 minutes

Cook Time: 12 minutes

Total Time: 27 minutes

Servings: 12 cookies

Difficulty Level: Easy

Nutrition Information

Calories per serving: 125 kcal

Protein: 7g

Carbohydrates: 14g

Fat: 5g

Fiber: 2g

Sugar: 6g
