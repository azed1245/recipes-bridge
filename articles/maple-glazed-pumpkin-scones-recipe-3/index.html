Maple Glazed Pumpkin Scones Recipe

There is nothing quite like the aroma of pumpkin and warm spices filling your kitchen on a crisp autumn morning. These Maple Glazed Pumpkin Scones are the ultimate seasonal treat, offering a tender, flaky texture that melts in your mouth with every bite. They strike the perfect balance between earthy pumpkin flavors and the rich, woody sweetness of pure maple syrup.

Servings: 8 scones
