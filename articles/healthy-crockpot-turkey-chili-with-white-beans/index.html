Healthy Crockpot Turkey Chili With White Beans

October 1, 2026

There is nothing more comforting than a warm bowl of chili waiting for you after a long day. This Healthy Crockpot Turkey Chili With White Beans is the ultimate “set it and forget it” meal that doesn’t compromise on flavor or nutrition. By swapping traditional beef for lean ground turkey and using fiber-rich white beans, you get a lighter version of the classic comfort food that still feels incredibly satisfying.

Recipe Information

Prep Time: 15 minutes

Cook Time: 6 hours

Total Time: 6 hours 15 minutes

Servings: 6

Difficulty Level: Easy

Nutrition Information

Calories per serving: 310 kcal

Protein: 28g

Carbohydrates: 34g

Fat: 7g

Fiber: 9g

Sugar: 4g

Sodium: 680mg
