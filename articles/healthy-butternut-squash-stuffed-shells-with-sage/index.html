Healthy Butternut Squash Stuffed Shells With Sage

October 1, 2026

As the air turns crisp and the leaves begin to change, there is nothing quite as comforting as a warm, cheesy pasta dish. These Healthy Butternut Squash Stuffed Shells With Sage offer a nutritious twist on the classic Italian-American comfort food. By swapping out some of the heavy cheese for creamy, roasted butternut squash, you get a meal that is rich in vitamins and fiber without sacrificing that indulgent texture we all crave. It is a beautiful, vibrant dish that brings the best flavors of the harvest season right to your dinner table.

Recipe Information

Prep Time: 20 minutes

Cook Time: 30 minutes

Total Time: 50 minutes

Servings: 6

Difficulty Level: Medium
