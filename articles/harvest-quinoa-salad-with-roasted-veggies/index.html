Harvest Quinoa Salad With Roasted Veggies

October 1, 2026

As the leaves begin to turn and the air gets crisp, there is nothing more satisfying than a bowl full of seasonal flavors. This Harvest Quinoa Salad with Roasted Veggies is the ultimate celebration of autumn produce. It combines the earthy nuttiness of quinoa with caramelized sweet potatoes and crispy Brussels sprouts to create a meal that is as beautiful as it is delicious.

Servings: 4

Difficulty Level: Easy
