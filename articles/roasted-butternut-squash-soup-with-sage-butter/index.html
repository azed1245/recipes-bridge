Roasted Butternut Squash Soup With Sage Butter

October 1, 2026

As the leaves turn gold and the air grows crisp, nothing beats a warm bowl of soup. This Roasted Butternut Squash Soup With Sage Butter is the ultimate comfort food for a chilly evening. By roasting the squash first, we unlock a deep, caramelized sweetness that you just can’t get from boiling. When you top it off with a drizzle of nutty brown butter and crispy sage leaves, it transforms a simple vegetable soup into a gourmet masterpiece.
