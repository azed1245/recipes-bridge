CrockpotBeef Stew With Potatoes And Carrots

October 1, 2026

There is something incredibly comforting about walking into a home that smells like a slow-cooked beef stew. This Crockpot Beef Stew with potatoes and carrots is the ultimate “set it and forget it” meal that yields incredibly tender meat and a rich, savory gravy. It is the perfect dinner for a chilly evening or a busy weekday when you want a home-cooked meal without spending hours over the stove.

Recipe Information

Prep Time: 20 minutes

Cook Time: 8 hours

Total Time: 8 hours 20 minutes

Servings: 6

Difficulty Level: Easy

Calories per serving: 385 kcal

Protein: 32g

Carbohydrates: 28g

Fat: 16g
