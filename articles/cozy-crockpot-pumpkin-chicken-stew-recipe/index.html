Cozy Crockpot Pumpkin Chicken Stew

As the crisp autumn air settles in and the leaves begin to turn vibrant shades of orange and gold, there is nothing more comforting than a warm, hearty meal waiting for you at home. This Cozy Crockpot Pumpkin Chicken Stew is the definition of fall in a bowl. It combines the earthy, subtle sweetness of pumpkin with tender chicken and savory herbs to create a dinner that is both nourishing and deeply satisfying.
